**The Problem DHS Faces:**
The Department of Human Settlements manages billions of Rands in housing projects annually but struggles with:
- **Cost overruns** on RDP/BNG housing (averaging 15-25% over budget)
- **Lack of pricing transparency** across South Africa's 9 provinces
- **Green building compliance** mandates without tracking tools
- **Supplier collusion** driving up costs
- **Manual BOQ pricing** taking weeks instead of minutes

**Qilly's Solution:**
An AI-powered construction billing system that **automatically prices Bills of Quantities** using live South African supplier data, tracks **green building metrics**, ensures **compliance**, and integrates with **eTender** for transparent project delivery.

## ⚠️ OBJECTION HANDLING

### **"We already have quantity surveyors who price BOQs"**
**Response:**  
"Absolutely, and Qilly makes them 100x more productive. Instead of spending 3 weeks calling suppliers, they upload a BOQ, verify Qilly's pricing, and use their expertise for value engineering and risk assessment. We're augmenting, not replacing."

### **"What about small, local suppliers not in your database?"**
**Response:**  
"Our 9-supplier network covers 87% of construction materials. For specialized items, quantity surveyors can manually override Qilly's suggestions. The system is hybrid by design—AI for commodities, human expertise for custom work."

### **"How accurate is your pricing data?"**
**Response:**  
"Our supplier quotes are from actual retailer catalogs (Builders, Cashbuild, Makro, etc.) updated monthly. We've validated against 2,500+ real BOQ items. Average pricing accuracy: ±3.5%, well within industry standards of ±5%."

### **"NEMA compliance is more complex than software can handle"**
**Response:**  
"Correct—Qilly doesn't replace environmental consultants. We screen for NEMA triggers (Basic Assessment vs EIA), estimate compliance costs, and flag timeline impacts. Then you engage specialists. But now you know upfront: 'This project needs R120k for NEMA authorization and adds 107 days.' No surprises."

### **"What about data security and PII?"**
**Response:**  
"Qilly is designed for non-PII construction data (BOQs, material prices, compliance costs). No personal information is stored. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. We can deploy on-premise for sensitive projects if required."
