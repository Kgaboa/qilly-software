### Red Flags to Watch For

**Bad Deal Signals:**

1. ❌ **Lowball offer with "take it or leave it" pressure**
   - Response: Walk away. Shows buyer doesn't value you.

2. ❌ **Excessive earn-out (>50% of purchase price)**
   - Response: Negotiate down to 30% or walk.

3. ❌ **Unrealistic earn-out milestones**
   - Example: "Hit R500M revenue in Year 1" (when current is R0)
   - Response: Negotiate realistic milestones based on your projections.

4. ❌ **Long earn-out period (>3 years)**
   - Response: Counter with 18-24 months.

5. ❌ **No employment agreement for founder**
   - Response: Require 2-year employment + vesting to protect earn-out.

6. ❌ **Integration plan that kills Qilly brand**
   - Response: Negotiate independent business unit status.

7. ❌ **Excessive due diligence period (>90 days)**
   - Response: Limit to 60 days with milestones.

8. ❌ **No breakup fee if buyer walks away**
   - Response: Negotiate 5-10% breakup fee for exclusivity.
