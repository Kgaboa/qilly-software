## Part 3: Solution 2 - Collusion Detection System (90 seconds)

**Transition:**
> "Now, the fraud problem. Let me show you how Qilly helps tender authorities detect bid rigging."

[Scroll to orange Collusion Detection card]

> "This is our Collusion Detection system. It validates your pricing against other tender submissions to identify suspicious patterns."

[Point to "How It Works" section]

> "Here's the methodology:
> 1. **Price Similarity Analysis** - We compare your BOQ total and line-item pricing against other submissions. If two bids are more than 80% similar, that's a red flag.
> 2. **Statistical Outlier Detection** - We identify abnormal deviations from the market average.
> 3. **Pattern Recognition** - We look for identical unit prices, matching rounding patterns, common supplier selections across multiple bids.
> 4. **Risk Scoring** - We flag high-risk submissions for tender authorities to investigate."

[Click "Run Collusion Detection Analysis"]

> "Let me run the analysis on this BOQ."

[Wait for dashboard to appear]

[Point to summary cards]

## Q&A Prep

### **Q: How long to integrate?**
**A:** Tender Response Generator: 2 months. Collusion Detection: 4 months (requires data pipeline setup).

### **Q: What if contractors don't want to pay?**
**A:** Freemium model. Basic BOQ pricing is free forever. Premium tier ($99/month) for tender generation. Only frequent bidders (8+ tenders/month) need Premium - that's 20-30% of your base. They'll pay because they're saving 4-6 hours PER TENDER.

### **Q: What about privacy for collusion detection?**
**A:** Full POPIA compliance. Contractor identities anonymized. Only tender authorities see flagged submissions. Audit trail for legal investigations.

### **Q: Can cartels game the system?**
**A:** Our algorithm looks at line-item pricing, not just totals. To evade detection, cartels would need to randomize EVERY BOQ item, which defeats the purpose of price coordination. Plus, we use pattern recognition (rounding, supplier selection) that's harder to fake.

### **Q: Why should eTender partner vs build this themselves?**
**A:** 
1. **Time:** We're live now. Building this would take eTender 12-18 months.
2. **Expertise:** We have BuildAid 2025/2026 compliance, carbon tracking, regional pricing - specialized domain knowledge.
3. **Risk:** We take development risk. You get proven product.
4. **Revenue:** Partnership generates income immediately. Building in-house requires upfront investment with uncertain ROI.
