## 🎤 Q&A PREP: Anticipated Questions

### **Q: How often is supplier data updated?**
**A:** Monthly for catalog prices. Weekly for availability. Daily for promotional pricing (Builders' specials, etc.). We validate against actual retailer websites and update the database accordingly.

### **Q: What if a supplier isn't in your system?**
**A:** Quantity surveyors can manually override any line item. Qilly is hybrid by design—AI for 87% of commodities, human expertise for custom/specialized work.

### **Q: How do you handle regional suppliers (small local businesses)?**
**A:** Our 9 major suppliers cover standard materials. For local suppliers (e.g., community brickyards), the QS adds them manually. We're building a "Local Supplier Database" feature for Phase 2 where municipalities can register community businesses.

### **Q: What about data security?**
**A:** Qilly handles non-PII construction data (BOQs, material prices). All data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. We can deploy on-premise for highly sensitive projects. No personal information is stored.

### **Q: How accurate is the collusion detection?**
**A:** We've validated against 2,500+ historical BOQs. Accuracy: 87% detection rate for known collusion cases. It's a screening tool—DHS investigators still review flagged tenders.

### **Q: Can this integrate with DHS's existing systems (SAP, etc.)?**
**A:** Yes. Qilly has REST APIs for two-way data exchange. We can push priced BOQs to SAP, pull project budgets, sync supplier databases. Standard enterprise integration.

### **Q: What about maintenance and support?**
**A:** 24/7 email/phone support. Monthly feature updates. Quarterly training webinars. Dedicated account manager for DHS. SLA: 99.9% uptime.

### **Q: Who built Qilly? Are you South African?**

Yes, Qilly is built by a South African team with 15+ years of construction and software experience. We understand BuildAid, NHBRC, CIDB, SANS standards because we've worked in the industry.
