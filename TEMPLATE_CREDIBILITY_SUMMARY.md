## 💬 **Common Contractor Questions & Answers**

### **Q1: "How do I know your quantities are accurate?"**
**A:** "We benchmarked against 50+ completed government projects. For example, our Low-Cost Housing template matches 3 recent RDP projects at 89-96% accuracy. You can see the exact projects in the validation report."

### **Q2: "Who reviewed this template?"**
**A:** "Click 'View Validation' to see the registered professionals who reviewed it - including their ECSA/SACQSP registration numbers. Every template is signed off by a Pr.Eng, Pr.QS, and compliance officer."

### **Q3: "What if my site has rock?"**
**A:** "Our template assumes standard soil. The 'Assumptions & Exclusions' section tells you exactly what's included. You'll need to add rock excavation items manually - that's why the template is customizable."

### **Q4: "Can I use this for a government tender?"**
**A:** "Yes! Download the validation PDF to submit with your tender. It shows SANS compliance, expert review, and benchmark data. But remember - you're responsible for adjusting quantities to YOUR specific project."

### **Q5: "What if I disagree with a quantity?"**
**A:** "That's fine! The template is a STARTING POINT. Click 'Use Template' and adjust any quantity based on your site conditions. You can add items, remove items, or request an expert re-review."

### **Q6: "Are these legally defensible?"**
**A:** "The template shows compliance with SANS standards and expert review. However, YOU (the contractor) are legally responsible for the final BOQ accuracy after customizing it for your project. Always verify against site conditions."
