### **2. Which sources are we basing this from if asked?**

**Primary Sources:**

1. **ICE Database v3.0** (University of Bath, UK)
   - Global standard for embodied carbon coefficients
   - Peer-reviewed, updated annually
   - Used by LEED, BREEAM, Green Star certifications
   - Link: https://circularecology.com/ice-database.html

2. **GBCSA** (Green Building Council South Africa)
   - DHS's official green building partner
   - South African-specific adjustments to global data
   - Link: https://www.gbcsa.org.za/

3. **SANS 10400-XA** (South African National Standard)
   - Energy efficiency in buildings regulation
   - Mandatory for DHS projects

4. **EPD Certifications** (Environmental Product Declarations)
   - Manufacturer-specific carbon data (PPC, AfriSam, etc.)
   - Third-party verified (ISO 14025 standard)

5. **Supplier Technical Documentation**
   - PPC CEM II spec sheets (ppc.co.za)
   - AfriSam Green Concrete data (afrisam.co.za)
   - ArcelorMittal Sustainability Report 2023
   - Corobrik environmental certifications
