## 2. The Problem

### Current Procurement Process Pain Points

#### 2.1 Government Sector Challenges

**Problem 1: Excessive Professional Fees**
- **Current State:** Quantity surveyors charge R15,000-R50,000 per BOQ
- **Impact:** R5.7 billion annually in professional fees for government housing
- **Root Cause:** Manual pricing requires specialized expertise (3-7 days work)
- **Consequence:** Budget overruns, fewer houses delivered

**Problem 2: Project Delays**
- **Current State:** BOQ pricing delays projects by 2-4 weeks minimum
- **Impact:** 47% of housing projects delayed, costing R12 billion annually
- **Root Cause:** Waiting for quantity surveyor quotes, back-and-forth iterations
- **Consequence:** Communities wait longer for housing, political pressure

**Problem 3: Procurement Fraud & Corruption**
- **Current State:** Opaque pricing allows inflated quotes (20-40% markups)
- **Impact:** R14.8 billion lost to construction procurement fraud annually
- **Root Cause:** No transparent price benchmarking, supplier collusion
- **Consequence:** Taxpayer money wasted, public trust eroded

**Problem 4: Compliance Complexity**
- **Current State:** Manual checking of SANS 1200, NBR, AGRÉMENT standards
- **Impact:** 23% of projects face compliance delays (avg 6 weeks)
- **Root Cause:** No integrated compliance verification system
- **Consequence:** Legal risks, project suspensions, penalties

**Problem 5: Regional Price Disparities**
- **Current State:** No visibility into regional price variations
- **Impact:** Projects overpay 15-30% by using distant suppliers
- **Root Cause:** No tools to compare regional pricing and transport costs
- **Consequence:** Budget inefficiency, environmental impact

#### 2.2 Private Sector Challenges

**Problem 1: Cost Estimation Accuracy**
- **Current State:** 62% of private projects exceed budget by 10-25%
- **Impact:** R45 billion in cost overruns annually
- **Root Cause:** Manual estimates based on outdated price lists
- **Consequence:** Project cancellations, contractor disputes

**Problem 2: Competitive Bidding Inefficiency**
- **Current State:** Contractors spend R8,000-R20,000 per tender bid
- **Impact:** 85% of bids unsuccessful, wasted costs
- **Root Cause:** Time-consuming manual BOQ pricing
- **Consequence:** Smaller contractors excluded from market

**Problem 3: BBBEE Compliance Tracking**
- **Current State:** Manual tracking of supplier transformation status
- **Impact:** Companies risk non-compliance penalties
- **Root Cause:** No automated BBBEE verification system
- **Consequence:** Lost government contracts, legal penalties

## 3. The Solution

### Qilly Platform Overview

Qilly is a cloud-based **Construction Billing System** that automates Bill of Quantities (BOQ) pricing using real-time supplier data, achieving professional-grade accuracy in under 5 minutes.

### Core Value Propositions

#### 3.1 Speed: 99% Time Reduction
- **Before:** 3-7 days for professional quantity surveyor
- **After:** <5 minutes automated pricing
- **Impact:** Eliminate project delays, accelerate procurement cycles

#### 3.2 Cost: 100% Fee Elimination
- **Before:** R15,000-R50,000 per BOQ in professional fees
- **After:** R500-R2,000 monthly subscription (unlimited BOQs)
- **Impact:** Save R5.7 billion annually across SA government housing

#### 3.3 Accuracy: 100% Pricing Confidence
- **Technology:** Multi-level intelligent product matching
- **Coverage:** 31 suppliers, ~105 products, 9 provinces
- **Validation:** Confidence scoring (0-100%) per match
- **Impact:** Professional-grade accuracy without human expertise

#### 3.4 Transparency: Anti-Corruption
- **Feature:** All prices sourced from verified suppliers
- **Audit:** Complete pricing audit trail (immutable)
- **Comparison:** Side-by-side supplier price comparison
- **Impact:** Prevent inflated quotes, detect fraud patterns

#### 3.5 Compliance: Built-In Standards
- **SANS 1200:** South African National Standards for construction
- **NBR:** National Building Regulations validation
- **AGRÉMENT:** Product certification verification
- **BBBEE:** Transformation tracking and reporting
- **Impact:** Eliminate compliance delays, reduce legal risk

#### 3.6 Regional Optimization: Provincial Coverage
- **Coverage:** All 9 SA provinces (EC, FS, GP, KZN, LP, MP, NC, NW, WC)
- **Transport:** Nearest supplier optimization
- **Savings:** 15-30% cost reduction through regional pricing
- **Impact:** Efficient resource allocation, environmental benefit
