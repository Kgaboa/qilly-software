## 🚨 **Potential Questions & Answers**

**Q: How do you prevent partners from competing with each other?**
> A: Partners are either white-label (different brands/markets) or construction firms (customers, not competitors). Software partners get exclusive territories where applicable.

**Q: What's your revenue split with white-label partners?**
> A: We keep 60-70%, partners get 30-40% commission. They handle sales/support, we provide the BOQ engine.

**Q: How long does partner onboarding take?**
> A: 2-4 weeks. API integration typically takes 1 week for experienced teams, white-label setup another week, then testing.

**Q: Do partners need technical resources?**
> A: For software partners, yes - they need developers. For construction firm partners, no - they just use the platform through their Custom tier account.
