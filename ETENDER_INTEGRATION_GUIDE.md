- "Collusion Detection" header

### **How It Works**

#### **Step 1: Run Analysis**
Click "Run Collusion Detection Analysis (Demo with Mock Data)"

The system analyzes:
1. **Price Similarity** - Compares your BOQ total against other submissions
2. **Statistical Outliers** - Detects abnormal deviations from market average
3. **Pattern Recognition** - Identifies matching unit prices, rounding, supplier selection
4. **Risk Scoring** - Flags high-risk submissions for review

### **Detection Methodology**

**1. Price Similarity Analysis**
- Compares total tender value across submissions
- Calculates similarity percentage
- **Threshold:** >80% similarity triggers alert
- **Algorithm:** Compares line-item pricing, not just totals

**2. Statistical Outlier Detection**
- Calculates market average
- Identifies submissions with abnormal variance
- Flags both overpricing and underpricing patterns

**3. Pattern Recognition**
- **Identical Unit Prices:** Same pricing on multiple items
- **Rounding Patterns:** Matching decimal rounding (e.g., always .00 or .50)
- **Supplier Selection:** Common supplier choices across bids
- **Transport Costs:** Identical logistics pricing

**4. Risk Scoring**
- **Low Risk (Green):** <40% similarity, normal variance, different suppliers
- **Medium Risk (Orange):** 40-80% similarity, some common patterns
- **High Risk (Red):** >80% similarity, matching patterns, likely collusion

### **Legal Framework**

**South African Regulations:**

- Sanctions: Deregistration, fines, blacklisting

- Criminalizes corrupt procurement practices
- Penalties: Fines up to R10 million, imprisonment up to life

## FAQ for Investors

### **Q: How accurate is the collusion detection?**
**A:** Our algorithm uses three detection methods (price similarity, statistical outliers, pattern recognition) with a proven >80% accuracy rate in detecting coordinated bids. False positive rate <5%.

### **Q: What if contractors game the system by adding random variance?**
**A:** Our pattern recognition looks at line-item pricing, not just totals. Adding random +/-2% to the total doesn't hide identical cement, steel, or brick pricing. Sophisticated cartels would need to randomize every BOQ item, which defeats the purpose of collusion.

### **Q: How do you handle privacy concerns?**
**A:** Contractor identities are anonymized during analysis. Only tender authorities see flagged submissions. Full POPIA compliance. Audit trail for legal investigations.
