## 🌟 The Story in 60 Seconds

**October 2023:** Penny Ndlovu, a software engineer from Johannesburg, asks her friend Zanele Mthembu, a civil engineer, for a simple Bill of Quantities (BOQ) quote.

**The Response:** "It'll take two weeks."

**Penny's Reaction:** "**Two weeks?!** For a pricing estimate?"

**Zanele's Reality:** "Welcome to construction. I'll call 50 suppliers, check 400-page compliance manuals, calculate transport costs manually, and hope my 2019 price lists are still accurate."

**The Eureka Moment:** Over coffee at Mugg & Bean in Midrand, Penny sketches on a napkin: "What if we automate this... in **5 minutes**?"

**November 2023:** Qilly is born.

**February 2026:** Qilly processes R3 million BOQs in **1.4 seconds**, has 31 suppliers across 9 provinces, and holds a Letter of Intent from the Department of Human Settlements.

**The Impact:** Qilly is positioned to save the South African government **R5.7 billion annually** in professional fees—enough to build **22,500 additional houses** per year.

## 💡 The Problem They Solve

**Government Procurement Crisis:**
- **R5.7 billion** wasted annually on quantity surveying fees
- **R14.8 billion** lost to procurement fraud and corruption
- **R12.3 billion** lost to project delays
- **47%** of government housing projects behind schedule
- **270,000 families** waiting for housing

**Current Process (Manual):**
- ⏰ **3-7 days** to price a BOQ
- 💰 **R15,000-R50,000** professional fees per project
- 📞 Calls to 20-50 suppliers (many don't answer)
- 📋 Manual SANS 1200, NBR, AGRÉMENT compliance checking
- ❌ No transparency (fraud thrives)
- ❌ No regional optimization (overpay 15-30%)

---

## ✅ The Qilly Solution

**Automated BOQ Pricing Platform:**
- ⚡ **5 minutes** to price any BOQ (vs 3-7 days)
- 💰 **R0 per project** on subscription (vs R15k-R50k)
- 🏆 **31 suppliers** across all 9 SA provinces
- 📦 **105+ products** in catalog
- ✅ **Automated compliance** (SANS 1200, NBR, AGRÉMENT, BBBEE)
- 🚚 **Transport optimization** (nearest supplier, lowest landed cost)
- 🔍 **100% transparency** (every price shown, audit trail logged)
- 🛡️ **Anti-corruption** (multi-supplier comparison, anomaly detection)

**Results:**
- 1.4-second processing time (proven in production)
- 7.2% average cost savings
- R1.68 million saved across 10 pilot projects
- 100% pricing accuracy with confidence scoring
