## 🛡️ Legal Safeguards We Included

**1. Clear Disclaimer:**
> "Qilly's environmental screening is an advisory tool based on publicly available NEMA regulations. It does NOT replace professional environmental assessment. Contractors should engage registered Environmental Assessment Practitioners (EAPs) for projects requiring Environmental Authorization."

**2. Always Recommend Licensed Professionals:**
- Action plans say: "Engage EAP registered with EAPASA"
- Link to EAPASA database: https://www.eapasa.org
- Never suggest "skip the EAP"

**3. Conservative Screening:**
- When in doubt, we flag for EAP review
- Better to over-flag than miss a requirement
- Protects contractors from non-compliance

**4. No Guarantees:**
- Don't claim "100% accurate"
- Don't say "Use Qilly, skip the EAP"
- Don't promise "EA approval guaranteed"

## 📊 Compliance Assessment Summary

### ✅ What Qilly DOES Comply With

1. **NEMA (Act 107 of 1998)**
   - Not conducting listed activities (software ≠ construction)
   - Helps users identify if EA is required
   - Recommends engagement with registered EAPs

2. **Waste Act (Act 59 of 2008)**
   - No construction waste generated (software)
   - Calculates waste estimates for construction projects
   - Reminds users to register with SAWIC

3. **POPIA (Act 4 of 2013)**
   - Consent forms implemented
   - Privacy policy live
   - Secure data storage (Supabase encryption)

4. **Consumer Protection Act**
   - Transparent pricing (R499/month)
   - Clear T&Cs
   - Refund policy

5. **Provincial Environmental Legislation**
   - Database of 9 provincial requirements
   - Links to provincial authorities
   - Flags province-specific triggers

---

### ❌ What Qilly Does NOT Need to Comply With

1. **EAPASA Registration**
   - Only required for individuals conducting EIAs
   - Not required for software tools
   - Qilly is not an EAP, doesn't claim to be

2. **Environmental Licenses**
   - Only required for activities (construction, waste management, air emissions)
   - Not required for software development/operation
   - Qilly doesn't conduct regulated activities

3. **SAHRA Heritage Permits**
   - Only required for activities disturbing heritage sites
   - Not required for heritage screening tools
   - Qilly flags need for HIA, doesn't conduct HIAs
