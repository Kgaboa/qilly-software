## Investor Questions & Answers

### **Q: "Is the green premium always added?"**
**A:** "No, it's optional. Contractors toggle 'Show Green Analysis' on or off. When off, they get standard pricing. When on, they see the green premium clearly labeled. Their choice."

### **Q: "How do you calculate cost per ton of CO₂ saved?"**
**A:** "Simple math: Green premium divided by carbon savings. In this example, R12,450 ÷ 15.3 tons = R814 per ton. Compare that to carbon offset markets at R200-400 per ton - this is better quality materials, not just credits."

### **Q: "Why include this in the overall total?"**
**A:** "Transparency and accuracy. If a contractor wants to go green, their tender price needs to reflect the true cost. We don't want surprises later. The overall total should be what they actually submit to clients."

### **Q: "What if they don't want green materials?"**
**A:** "They don't use it. But 65% of government tenders now include environmental compliance scoring. Contractors who present carbon analysis win more contracts. Even if they don't care about the environment, they care about winning tenders."
