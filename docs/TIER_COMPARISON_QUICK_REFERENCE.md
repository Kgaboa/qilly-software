## 🎤 INVESTOR PRESENTATION SOUNDBITES

### Opening (Problem)
> "Construction billing in South Africa is broken. Contractors spend 20+ hours pricing a single BOQ, pay R5,000-R15,000 for quantity surveyors, and still face 10-15% pricing errors. Our target customer—government contractors—also need carbon tracking for DHS compliance, but no solution exists."

### Solution
> "Qilly automates BOQ pricing using live supplier data, reducing 20 hours of work to under 5 minutes. Our ENTERPRISE tier includes carbon tracking—critical for Department of Human Settlements contracts—making us the only solution in the South African market with this capability."

### Business Model
> "We use a strategic 4-tier model. FREE tier is training only—all pricing encrypted—forcing contractors to upgrade for production work. PROFESSIONAL at R2,999/month serves individual contractors. ENTERPRISE at R8,999/month unlocks carbon tracking and eTender integration for government work. This creates a natural upgrade path as contractors grow."

### Market Positioning
> "Our ENTERPRISE tier is specifically designed for the DHS and government contracts. With built-in carbon tracking, green building features, and eTender integration, we're positioning Qilly as the compliance-first platform for sustainable construction in South Africa."

### Competitive Advantage
> "Our FREE tier isn't freemium—it's a moat. Competitors can't scrape our pricing because all amounts are encrypted. Contractors learn our workflow with real BuildAid templates, then must upgrade for production. Combined with carbon tracking, we're not just a billing tool—we're an environmental compliance platform."

### Call to Action
> "We're ready to partner with eTender to become the default BOQ pricing solution for South African government contracts. Our ENTERPRISE tier's carbon tracking aligns perfectly with DHS sustainability goals. Let's discuss integration."
