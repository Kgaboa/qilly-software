## ✅ WHAT QILLY **FULLY AUTOMATES** (60% of QS Time)

### **1. Supplier Quotation Management**
**Traditional Process:**
- QS sends email to 5-10 suppliers with BOQ attached
- Wait 3-7 days for responses
- Manual Excel comparison of quotes
- Phone calls to clarify pricing

**Qilly Process:**
- Upload BOQ → Instant quotes from 9 suppliers
- Side-by-side comparison automatically displayed
- **Time saved: 3 weeks → 30 seconds (99% reduction)**

---

### **2. Transport Cost Calculation**
**Traditional Process:**
- Google Maps each supplier branch to site
- Manually calculate distance × R/km rate
- Apply fuel surcharges, toll costs
- Different rates for different materials

**Qilly Process:**
- Automatic branch-to-site distance calculation
- Material-specific transport rates applied
- Nearest branch auto-selected
- **Time saved: 2-4 hours → instant**

---

### **3. Regional Pricing Adjustments**
**Traditional Process:**
- Research provincial cost multipliers
- Apply Western Cape +40%, Limpopo -20%, etc.
- Adjust for municipal demand (Johannesburg vs Rustenburg)
- Manual Excel formulas prone to errors

**Qilly Process:**
- 9-province pricing engine with real multipliers
- Municipal-level adjustments automatic
- Based on live supplier branch data
- **Time saved: 1-2 hours → instant**

---

### **4. Compliance Cost Calculation**
**Traditional Process:**
- Calculate NHBRC enrollment: Units × R850
- Calculate NHBRC inspections: Units × 5 visits × R450
- Calculate 10-year warranty: Units × R1,800
- Calculate CIDB levies, UIF, SDL, COIDA, pension
- Research NEMA triggers, waste disposal costs
- Manual spreadsheet with 50+ formulas

**Qilly Process:**
- All compliance costs calculated automatically:
  - NHBRC (enrollment, inspections, warranty)
  - CIDB (grade validation, levies)
  - Statutory labour (UIF, SDL, COIDA, pension)
  - Testing (SANS 2001, 227, 3001)
  - BBBEE verification
  - Environmental (NEMA, waste, provincial)
- **Time saved: 3-5 hours → instant**

---

## ⚠️ WHAT QILLY **PARTIALLY ASSISTS** (20% of QS Time)

### **1. Value Engineering**
**Human QS Role:** Analyze alternatives, recommend cost-saving substitutions, balance quality vs budget  
**Qilly Role:** Suggests cheaper suppliers, shows green alternatives, flags expensive items  
**Impact:** QS makes final decisions with Qilly data

### **2. BOQ Review & Validation**
**Human QS Role:** Verify quantities are correct, check for missing items, ensure compliance  
**Qilly Role:** Flags unusual pricing, highlights cost outliers, validates compliance  
**Impact:** QS spot-checks Qilly's work (like a senior QS reviewing a junior's estimate)

### **3. Variation Order Pricing**
**Human QS Role:** Assess scope changes, negotiate with client, justify pricing  
**Qilly Role:** Reprice changed items instantly using updated quantities  
**Impact:** QS focuses on negotiation, Qilly handles calculations

### **4. Risk Assessment**
**Human QS Role:** Identify project risks, recommend contingencies, advise on procurement strategy  
**Qilly Role:** Shows price volatility, supplier availability, collusion patterns  
**Impact:** QS uses Qilly data to inform risk decisions

---

## ❌ WHAT QILLY **CANNOT DO** (20% of QS Time)

### **1. Measurement & Take-Off**
**Why Qilly Can't Do This:**
- Requires reading architectural/engineering drawings
- Requires professional judgment (wall thickness, rebar spacing, etc.)
- Requires CAD skills (AutoCAD, Revit, CostX)
- Complex calculations (curved walls, irregular shapes, slopes)

**QS Remains Essential:** Qilly starts AFTER quantities are determined

---

### **2. Client Relationships & Negotiation**
**Why Qilly Can't Do This:**
- Requires trust-building, communication skills
- Requires understanding client priorities (cost vs quality vs speed)
- Requires negotiation expertise (pricing, payment terms, variations)
- Requires conflict resolution (disputes, claims)

**QS Remains Essential:** Qilly provides data, QS provides advice

---

### **3. Contract Administration**
**Why Qilly Can't Do This:**
- Requires legal knowledge (JBCC, FIDIC, NEC contracts)
- Requires site inspections, progress assessments
- Requires payment certification, retention management
- Requires final account negotiation, dispute resolution

**QS Remains Essential:** Qilly doesn't replace the QS's legal/contractual role

---

### **4. Professional Judgment**
**Why Qilly Can't Do This:**
- Deciding when to use cheaper material vs higher quality
- Assessing contractor capabilities (can they deliver at this price?)
- Recommending project delivery method (traditional, design-build, etc.)
- Advising on cash flow, financing, project phasing

**QS Remains Essential:** Qilly is a tool, not a professional advisor
