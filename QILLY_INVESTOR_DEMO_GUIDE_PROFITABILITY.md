## Objection Handling

### Common Objections & Responses

#### Objection 1: "What if suppliers don't keep their data updated?"

**Response:**
"Great question. We've addressed this three ways:

1. **Contractual Obligation:** Our supplier agreements require weekly price updates. Non-compliance = suspension from platform.

2. **Supplier Incentive:** Suppliers WANT to be on Qilly because we send them R500M+ in order opportunities per year. They're motivated to keep prices accurate.

3. **Validation Mechanism:** If a supplier's price is flagged as inaccurate by a customer, we immediately:
   - Contact supplier to verify
   - Suspend listing if not corrected within 24 hours
   - Track accuracy score per supplier

Current accuracy rate: 98.7% (6 price disputes out of 450 projects)"

---

#### Objection 2: "Government procurement is slow. How will you sell to them?"

**Response:**
"You're absolutely right - typical government sales can take 18-36 months. But Qilly is different:

1. **Funding Proposal Approach:** We're not selling to procurement departments. We're partnering with Department of Human Settlements as a **funded solution** to their R5.7B professional fees problem. They GIVE us funding to build this because it saves them money.

2. **Policy Alignment:** Government is ACTIVELY SEEKING anti-corruption tech. POPIA compliance. Transparency. We tick every box.

3. **Executive Sponsorship:** We have a Letter of Intent from the Director-General level - not a mid-level manager. This accelerates procurement.

4. **No RFP Needed:** As a funded development project, we bypass traditional RFP processes.

Expected timeline: 6-9 months from LOI to first R5M payment. We're in month 4."

4. **Junior Staff Replacement:** QS firms spend 80% of junior QS time on price research. That's R800k/year per junior QS. Qilly costs R120k/year. They save R680k per junior.
