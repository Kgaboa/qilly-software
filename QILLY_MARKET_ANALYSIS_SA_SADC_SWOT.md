## Executive Summary

**Market Opportunity:**  
The South African construction procurement market represents a R400+ billion annual opportunity, with government infrastructure spend accounting for R180+ billion. The SADC construction market totals approximately R1.2 trillion annually.

**Key Findings:**
- ✅ **No direct competitors** offering automated, multi-province BOQ pricing in SA
- ✅ **Fragmented market** with manual processes still dominant
- ✅ **High government demand** for transparency and cost optimization
- ✅ **Strong regulatory tailwinds** (POPIA, anti-corruption, transformation)
- ✅ **SADC expansion potential** with similar procurement challenges

**Competitive Positioning:**  
Qilly is positioned as the **first-to-market automated BOQ pricing platform** specifically designed for South African construction standards (SANS 1200, NBR, AGRÉMENT) with built-in compliance and anti-corruption features.

**Market Entry Strategy:**  
Government-first approach targeting Department of Human Settlements (R20+ billion annual budget) before expanding to private sector and SADC markets.
