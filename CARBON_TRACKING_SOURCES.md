## 📊 Carbon Data Sources (Scientifically Validated)

### 1. **ICE Database v3.0** (University of Bath, UK)
- **What:** Inventory of Carbon & Energy - industry-standard embodied carbon database
- **Usage:** Base carbon coefficients for construction materials (cement, steel, concrete)
- **Credibility:** Most widely cited embodied carbon database globally, peer-reviewed
- **Link:** https://circularecology.com/ice-database.html
- **Status:** ✅ Industry standard for carbon calculations

### 2. **GBCSA** (Green Building Council South Africa)
- **What:** Local green building certification body (affiliated with World GBC)
- **Usage:** South African-specific adjustments and green building standards
- **Credibility:** Official DHS partner for green building compliance
- **Link:** https://www.gbcsa.org.za/
- **Status:** ✅ Recognized by DHS and private sector

### 3. **SANS 10400-XA (2011)**
- **What:** South African National Standard for Energy Efficiency in Buildings
- **Usage:** Regulatory framework for sustainable construction
- **Credibility:** National building regulation (mandatory for DHS projects)
- **Status:** ✅ Legal requirement for government tenders

### 4. **EPD (Environmental Product Declarations)**
- **What:** Manufacturer-specific carbon footprint documentation (ISO 14025)
- **Usage:** Verified data from SA manufacturers (PPC, AfriSam, ArcelorMittal)
- **Credibility:** Third-party verified carbon data
- **Status:** ✅ Available from major SA suppliers

### 5. **Concrete Institute of South Africa**
- **What:** Industry body for concrete standards and research
- **Usage:** Local concrete carbon data and mix design specifications
- **Link:** https://www.concretesociety.co.za/
- **Status:** ✅ Industry-recognized authority

### 6. **Steel & Engineering Industries Federation of SA**
- **What:** Steel industry association with emissions data
- **Usage:** Carbon coefficients for SA-produced steel
- **Status:** ✅ Industry-validated
