## 📢 2-Minute Version (Podcast/Radio Interview)

**"Thanks for having me. I'm [Penny/Zanele], and I want to tell you how a two-week delay changed everything.**

**It's October 2023. Penny Ndlovu—a software engineer from Johannesburg—calls me, her friend Zanele Mthembu. I'm a civil engineer running a small consultancy in Midrand.**

**Penny says: 'Zee, I need a Bill of Quantities for a 100-unit housing project. How long will it take?'**

**I say: 'About two weeks.'**

**She goes: 'TWO WEEKS?! For a price quote?!'**

**I laugh because everyone in construction knows this is normal. But Penny's a tech person—she's used to instant results. So she's like, 'Can I come watch you work? I need to understand why.'**

**Next day, she's in my office. And for two hours, she watches me:**

**Call suppliers. Most don't answer. Leave voicemails. Check outdated price lists from 2019. Google transport distances manually. Cross-reference 400-page SANS 1200 compliance manuals. Verify AGRÉMENT certificates on a website that keeps crashing.**

**After one hour, I'd priced ONE item. I had 46 to go.**

**Penny looks at me and says: 'This is insane. Why isn't there a system?'**

**I shrug. 'This is how the industry works. Manual. Slow. Expensive. Everyone knows it's broken, but nobody's fixed it.'**

**Then I tell her about the fraud. How quantity surveyors inflate prices 20-40% and get kickbacks from suppliers. How R14.8 billion disappears into corruption every year. How local Black-owned suppliers get excluded because the 'network' is closed.**

**Penny goes quiet. Then she says: 'What if we built a platform that automates this? Upload your BOQ, get instant pricing from all suppliers, check compliance automatically, optimize transport costs, prevent fraud with transparency.'**

**That evening, we're at Mugg & Bean in Midrand. Penny's sketching on a napkin. I'm listing requirements: supplier database, compliance checks, BBBEE tracking, audit trails.**

**We call it Qilly. Short for 'Quantity, Easily.'**

**Fast forward 15 months: We've built it. We've tested it. We've proven it saves 7.2% on average. The City of Johannesburg signed a R250k/year contract. The Department of Human Settlements gave us a Letter of Intent.**

**Our platform processes a R3 million BOQ in 1.4 seconds. We have 31 suppliers across all 9 provinces. We've saved R1.68 million across 10 pilot projects.**

**Now we're raising R25 million to scale nationally. Because here's the thing: the government spends R5.7 billion annually on quantity surveying fees. Qilly eliminates 90% of that. That's R5 billion saved—enough to build 22,500 additional houses per year.**

**For 270,000 families waiting for housing, that's not just efficiency. That's hope.**

**Two Black women. A napkin sketch. And a mission to prove that transparency beats corruption.**

**That's the Qilly story."**
