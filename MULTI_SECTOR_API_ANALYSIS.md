## 🏗️ WHERE THE 6 COMPLIANCE FEATURES ACTUALLY APPLY

### **Feature 1: SANS 1200 (SA National Standard for Construction)**
**Applies To:**
- ✅ **National Government:** All departments (DHS, DoE, DoH, DPW, etc.)
- ✅ **Provincial Government:** All provincial construction projects
- ✅ **Municipalities:** All municipal infrastructure & housing (metro, local, district)
- ✅ **State-Owned Enterprises:** Eskom, Transnet, PRASA, etc.
- ✅ **Private Sector:** Large commercial/industrial projects often specify SANS 1200
- ✅ **Parastatals:** SANRAL, Water Boards, etc.

**Real-World Examples:**
- **Municipality:** Johannesburg Metro building RDP houses → SANS 1200 compliance required
- **Provincial Govt:** Western Cape Dept of Education building schools → SANS 1200 required
- **National Govt:** Dept of Public Works building government offices → SANS 1200 required
- **Private Sector:** Shopping mall developer → May specify SANS 1200 for quality assurance

---

### **Feature 2: NBR (National Building Regulations)**
**Applies To:**
- ✅ **ALL CONSTRUCTION IN SOUTH AFRICA** (mandatory by law)
- ✅ National Government
- ✅ Provincial Government
- ✅ Municipalities
- ✅ Private Sector
- ✅ SOEs and Parastatals

**Legal Basis:**
- National Building Regulations and Building Standards Act, 1977 (Act No. 103 of 1977)
- **Enforced by:** Local authorities (municipalities)
- **Applies to:** Every building, regardless of who builds it

**Real-World Examples:**
- **Municipality:** eThekwini building a community hall → NBR Parts A-V compliance required
- **Provincial Govt:** Gauteng Dept of Health building clinic → NBR compliance required
- **Private Developer:** Residential estate in Cape Town → NBR compliance required by municipality

---

### **Feature 3: AGRÉMENT South Africa Certification**
**Applies To:**
- ✅ **Any project using innovative/non-traditional materials**
- ✅ National Government
- ✅ Provincial Government
- ✅ Municipalities
- ✅ Private Sector
- ✅ SOEs and Parastatals

**Real-World Examples:**
- **Municipality:** Nelson Mandela Bay using fibre-cement cladding → AGRÉMENT required
- **DHS:** Low-cost housing using alternative roofing → AGRÉMENT required
- **Private Sector:** Commercial building using new waterproofing system → AGRÉMENT often required

---

### **Feature 4: POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act)**
**Applies To:**
- ✅ **EVERY ORGANIZATION IN SOUTH AFRICA** (law since July 2020)
- ✅ National Government
- ✅ Provincial Government
- ✅ Municipalities
- ✅ Private Sector
- ✅ SOEs and Parastatals
- ✅ NGOs and Non-Profits

**Legal Basis:**
- Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (Act No. 4 of 2013)
- **Enforced by:** Information Regulator
- **Penalty:** Up to R10 million or 10 years imprisonment

**Real-World Examples:**
- **Supplier to Municipality:** Must comply with POPIA when handling municipal procurement data
- **Supplier to DHS:** Must comply with POPIA when handling DHS project data
- **Supplier to Private Contractor:** Must comply with POPIA when handling contractor data

**CRITICAL:** This is **NOT** DHS-specific - it's a **national data privacy law**

---

### **Feature 5: BBBEE (Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment)**
**Applies To:**
- ✅ **All Government Procurement** (mandatory via PPPFA)
  - National Government
  - Provincial Government
  - Municipalities
  - SOEs and Parastatals
- ⚠️ **Private Sector:** Optional (but many large companies have BEE procurement policies)

**Legal Basis:**
- **PPPFA (Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Act), 2000** - applies to **ALL government**
- **B-BBEE Act, 2003 (as amended by Act 46 of 2013)**

**PPPFA Thresholds (applies to municipalities too!):**
- Contracts **up to R50 million:** 80 points price, 20 points BEE
- Contracts **above R50 million:** 90 points price, 10 points BEE

**Municipality-Specific:**
- **MFMA Section 112:** Municipalities must comply with PPPFA
- **Target:** 60%+ of procurement value to BEE-compliant suppliers (same as DHS!)

**Real-World Examples:**
- **Johannesburg Metro:** R20M road construction → 20 points allocated to BEE
- **Western Cape Provincial Govt:** R100M school building → 10 points allocated to BEE
- **DHS:** R15M housing project → 20 points allocated to BEE

**CRITICAL:** BBBEE applies to **ALL government entities**, not just DHS!

---

### **Feature 6: PFMA/MFMA Audit Trail Support**
**Applies To:**

| Act | Applies To | Procurement Auditing |
|-----|-----------|---------------------|
| **PFMA** (Public Finance Management Act, 1999) | • National Government Departments<br>• Provincial Government Departments<br>• National Public Entities<br>• Provincial Public Entities | ✅ 7-year audit trail required<br>✅ Auditor-General oversight<br>✅ National Treasury regulations |
| **MFMA** (Municipal Finance Management Act, 2003) | • Metropolitan Municipalities (8)<br>• Local Municipalities (205)<br>• District Municipalities (44)<br>• Municipal Entities | ✅ 7-year audit trail required<br>✅ Auditor-General oversight<br>✅ Municipal SCM regulations |

**Municipality Examples:**
- **City of Cape Town (Metro):** MFMA applies → audit trail required
- **Tshwane Metro:** MFMA applies → audit trail required
- **Polokwane Local Municipality:** MFMA applies → audit trail required
- **Ugu District Municipality:** MFMA applies → audit trail required

**CRITICAL:** Qilly's audit trail feature serves **BOTH** PFMA and MFMA!
