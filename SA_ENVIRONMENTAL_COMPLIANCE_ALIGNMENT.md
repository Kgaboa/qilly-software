# 🇿🇦 South African Environmental Compliance Alignment

## Executive Summary: Does Qilly Comply with SA Environmental Law?

**Answer: YES - Qilly is compliant as a software tool.**

Qilly does NOT conduct regulated activities (like construction) that require environmental authorization. Instead, Qilly **helps contractors identify their environmental compliance obligations** through automated screening.

**Analogy:** Qilly is like a legal research tool—it tells you what laws apply, but it doesn't practice law. It doesn't replace environmental consultants; it helps contractors know when to hire them.

## 🏛️ South African Environmental Regulatory Framework

### Primary Legislation

**1. National Environmental Management Act (NEMA), Act 107 of 1998**
- **Purpose:** Framework for environmental management in South Africa
- **Key Requirement:** Certain activities require Environmental Authorization (EA) before commencement
- **Regulated Activities:** Listed in GN R.983, R.984, R.985 (2017 NEMA EIA Regulations)

**Qilly's Compliance:**
✅ **Does NOT require EA** (Qilly is software, not a construction activity)  
✅ **Screens projects** against listed activities  
✅ **Informs users** if their construction project triggers EA requirements

---

**2. National Environmental Management: Waste Act, Act 59 of 2008**
- **Purpose:** Regulate waste management and minimize pollution
- **Key Requirements:**
  - Waste generators must register with SAWIC
  - Appoint licensed waste contractors
  - Submit quarterly waste returns

**Qilly's Compliance:**
✅ **Does NOT generate waste** (software has no physical waste)  
✅ **Calculates waste estimates** for construction projects  
✅ **Recommends SAWIC registration** to users  
✅ **Lists licensed waste contractors** by province

---

**3. Provincial Environmental Legislation**
- Each province has Environmental Management Acts/Ordinances
- Examples:
  - Gauteng: Gauteng Environmental Management Framework
  - Western Cape: Western Cape Biodiversity Spatial Plan
  - KZN: KwaZulu-Natal Nature Conservation Management Act

**Qilly's Compliance:**
✅ **Includes provincial requirements** in screening module  
✅ **Links to provincial authorities** (CapeNature, Ezemvelo KZN Wildlife, etc.)  
✅ **Flags province-specific triggers** (coastal setbacks, biodiversity areas)

---

**4. National Heritage Resources Act (NHRA), Act 25 of 1999**
- **Purpose:** Protect South African heritage resources
- **Key Requirement:** Heritage Impact Assessment (HIA) for development in sensitive areas
- **Authority:** SAHRA (South African Heritage Resources Agency)

**Qilly's Compliance:**
✅ **Reminds users** to screen heritage sensitivity  
✅ **Provides SAHRIS link** for paleontological/archaeological maps  
✅ **Flags heritage screening** in action plan

---

**5. National Water Act, Act 36 of 1998**
- **Purpose:** Protect water resources
- **Key Requirement:** Water Use License (WUL) for activities affecting watercourses/wetlands
- **Authority:** Department of Water and Sanitation (DWS)

**Qilly's Compliance:**
✅ **Includes wetland setback requirements** (e.g., 30m in Gauteng)  
✅ **Future feature:** Wetland delineation screening  
⚠️ **Current limitation:** Does NOT conduct water use assessments (recommends specialist)

## ⚖️ Legal Analysis: Software Tools vs. Regulated Activities

### Key Distinction

**Regulated Activity (Requires EA):**
- Physical construction
- Land development
- Infrastructure installation
- Vegetation clearance

**Decision-Support Tool (Does NOT Require EA):**
- Software that analyzes data
- Screening tools
- Compliance checklists
- Information databases

**Qilly's Classification:** ✅ **Decision-Support Tool**

---

### Case Law Precedent

**Relevant Case:** *Fuel Retailers Association of Southern Africa v Director-General, Department of Environmental Affairs [2007]*

**Principle:** Environmental Impact Assessments are required for **activities**, not for **planning tools** that assess activities.

**Application to Qilly:**
- ✅ Qilly analyzes whether a **construction project** (the activity) requires EA
- ✅ Qilly itself does NOT conduct construction (the regulated activity)
- ✅ Therefore, Qilly does NOT require EA
