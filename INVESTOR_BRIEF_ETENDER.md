## Product Features

### **Feature 1: Automated Tender Response Generator**

**What It Does:**
Converts a priced BOQ into a professional, multi-page PDF tender submission in 2 seconds.

**7 Auto-Generated Sections:**
1. ✅ Cover Page (company details, CIDB, BBBEE, tender value)
2. ✅ Executive Summary (project highlights, commitments)
3. ✅ Company Credentials (certifications, registrations)
4. ✅ Pricing Summary (BOQ + compliance + P&G + green premium)
5. ✅ Environmental Impact Statement (carbon savings, DHS score)
6. ✅ Project Timeline & Methodology
7. ✅ Declarations & Undertakings (anti-collusion pledge)

**Value Proposition:**
- **Time Saved:** 4-6 hours → 10 minutes (35x faster)
- **Quality:** Professional formatting, no errors, compliance-complete
- **Competitive Advantage:** Submit MORE tenders = MORE wins

**Revenue Model:**

- **Target:** Frequent bidders (8+ tenders/month) = 20-30% of contractor base
- **Projected Revenue:** **R350k/month** at 20% adoption (500 contractors)

### **Feature 2: Collusion Detection System**

**What It Does:**
Validates tender submissions against market data to detect bid rigging and price fixing.

**4 Detection Methods:**
1. **Price Similarity Analysis** - Flags >80% identical pricing
2. **Statistical Outlier Detection** - Identifies abnormal variance
3. **Pattern Recognition** - Detects matching unit prices, rounding, suppliers
4. **Risk Scoring** - Assigns LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH risk levels

**Value Proposition:**
- **For Government:** Detect fraud BEFORE awarding contracts (currently detected after)
- **For Honest Contractors:** Prove legitimacy of pricing, level playing field
- **For eTender:** Anti-fraud feature differentiates platform from competitors

**Legal Framework:**
- Competition Act 89 of 1998 (penalties up to 10% of annual turnover)
- CIDB Code of Conduct (deregistration for bid rigging)
- Prevention of Corrupt Activities Act 12 of 2004

**Revenue Model:**
- **Enterprise Tier:** R2,000/month per municipality
- **Target:** 30% of 70 major municipalities + provincial/national departments
- **Projected Revenue:** **R500k/month** at 30% government adoption

## Risks & Mitigation

### **Risk 1: eTender Won't Share Submission Data**

**Mitigation:**
- Use public benchmarks (CIDB pricing guides, BuildAid standards)
- Historical tender data from municipal archives
- Less accurate, but still valuable for outlier detection
- Position as "Market Intelligence" feature for contractors

### **Risk 2: Low Contractor Adoption**

**Mitigation:**
- Freemium model (basic BOQ pricing free forever)
- Free trial for 30 days (first 2 tenders free)
- ROI calculator: "See how much time Qilly saves you"
- Referral program: 1 month free for every contractor referred

### **Risk 3: Cartels Adapt to Evade Detection**

**Mitigation:**
- Machine learning: Algorithm improves with more data
- Multi-factor detection (price + patterns + suppliers + rounding)
- To evade, cartels must randomize EVERY BOQ item (defeats collusion purpose)
- Continuous updates based on Competition Commission investigation patterns

### **Risk 4: Government Procurement Cycles (Slow Sales)**

**Mitigation:**
- Focus on municipalities first (faster decision-making)
- Pilot programs (low risk, high visibility)
- Publish case studies immediately (show ROI)
- National Treasury endorsement (fast-track adoption)
